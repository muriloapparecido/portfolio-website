Overview

IronCircle is a mobile app designed to connect people who share similar fitness interests. The app allows users to match with workout partners based on their gym preferences, workout routines, frequency, and experience levels. With IronCircle, the goal is to make going to the gym more enjoyable and collaborative by fostering connections among like-minded individuals.

What I Built

  • Designed full UI wireframes and implemented swipe-based partner discovery with React Native and Expo.
  • Built real-time chat functionality using Supabase, supporting live message delivery between matched users.
  • Implemented user authentication and deep linking via Supabase Auth.
An early exploration project that laid the foundation for the more production-ready apps that followed.
